Frequently Asked Questions

Verified facts for media, institutional partners, journalists, and audiences

01 Who is Jean-Pierre Lofumbwa (Black_Pater / « Le Prof »)?
Jean-Pierre Lofumbwa is a Congolese-American educator, content creator, multilingual translator, and cultural ambassador based in Salt Lake City, Utah. Known globally as Black_Pater and nicknamed « Le Prof » by his community, he combines educational instruction, African trending culture, and life guidance for hundreds of thousands of followers worldwide.
02 What is his academic record in the Democratic Republic of Congo?
In 2015, Jean-Pierre Lofumbwa graduated with 85% in Latin-Philosophy, officially ranking as the #1 best student across the entire Democratic Republic of Congo. This exceptional achievement opened the pathway for him to pursue higher education in the United States starting in 2021.
03 Which media publications have featured Black_Pater?
He has been highlighted in US media including VoyageUtah Magazine ("Rising Stars: Meet Black_Pater Lofumbwa of Salt Lake City") and acclaimed analytical publications such as Béni Muwawa's editorial ("Black Pater : Le soldat digital du Congo").
04 What languages does Black_Pater speak and teach?
He speaks 5 languages fluently: French, English, Lingala, Swahili, and Tshiluba. He is currently completing his official professional translation credentials in French-English while offering language tips to his online students.
05 Why is he known as « Le Prof » (The Professor) / « Molakisi »?
His global community titled him « Le Prof » (or Molakisi in Lingala) because he is recognized as an authentic public educator. Rather than superficial trends, his platform delivers structured insights into current events, immigrant resilience, and cultural wisdom.
