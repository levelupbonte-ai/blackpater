TheProfessor

Jean-Pierre Lofumbwa is Black_Pater. His community calls him Le Prof — and he also answers to MOLAKISI.

EntrepreneurBuilding his own platform and projects.

Content creatorA large community on TikTok and beyond.

EducatorTeaching and translation are part of who he is.

Cultural ambassadorA Congolese voice, based in the United States.

His content blends

  • Humor

    The way in.

  • Culture

    Congolese and African, worn with pride.

  • Education

    Teaching is the thread.

  • Current events

    Read, explained, shared.

  • Dance

    Rhythm that travels.

  • Storytelling

    Life, told in his own voice.

  • Life experiences

    Migration, studies, starting over.

He uses his platforms to celebrate Congolese and African culture, share his own experience, and build a bridge between the diaspora and the culture it comes from.
